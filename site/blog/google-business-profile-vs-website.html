Do you need a website if you have a Google Business Profile?

Why home service businesses need both a Google Business Profile and a website, how they work together, and what to fix first.

By the SitesThatBook team . Updated October 2026 . 5 min read

Short answer: Yes. Your Google Business Profile gets you seen in the map results, but your website is what Google uses to confirm your services and location, and it is where most homeowners go to decide whether to trust you. The two work best together.
In this article
  1. What each one does
  2. How they help each other
  3. What to fix first

What each one does

Your Google Business Profile is your listing in Google Maps and the map pack, the three businesses that show under a map for searches like "plumber near me". It shows your reviews, hours, photos and a call button.

Your website is where homeowners go when they want more before they call: what you specialize in, whether you serve their town, what other customers say and whether you look like a real, established company.

How they help each other

  • Google reads your website to confirm the services and areas listed on your profile.
  • Matching name, phone and service area across both builds trust with Google.
  • Your profile sends visitors to your website, where a good site turns them into calls and booking requests.
  • Your website ranks in the regular results below the map, giving you a second shot at the same search.

What to fix first

  1. Make sure your profile is verified, in the right primary category and lists every service.
  2. Get a fast, mobile friendly website that lists the same services and service area.
  3. Link your website from your profile.
  4. Ask every happy customer for a Google review.
  5. Post on your profile regularly and publish helpful articles on your site.
