Questions

How much is GoDaddy Website Builder?

GoDaddy offers a free plan with GoDaddy branding. Independent reviews in 2026 list paid plans roughly between $10 and $30 a month depending on the plan and whether you pay yearly. Check GoDaddy's pricing page for current prices.

Can I keep my GoDaddy domain?

Yes. You keep your domain at GoDaddy and we point it to your new site.

Is a GoDaddy site good enough for a contractor?

For a basic online presence, it can be. To compete in local search, you need detailed service pages, city content and regular updates, which takes time to do yourself.
