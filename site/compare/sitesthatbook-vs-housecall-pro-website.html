Housecall Pro website vs SitesThatBook

Both are done-for-you. Housecall Pro builds and manages a site as an add-on to its software. SitesThatBook is a website service on its own. Here is how they differ.

Short answer: Housecall Pro Websites is a good fit if you run your business on Housecall Pro and want the site tied into its booking, chat and reviews. SitesThatBook is a better fit if you want public, flat pricing, a site that does not depend on your field service software, and monthly SEO content included.

Choose Housecall Pro if

  • You run your business on Housecall Pro and want online booking built into the site.
  • You want one vendor for software and website.
  • You do not mind requesting edits through their team.

Choose SitesThatBook if

  • You want to know the full price before you start: $349 setup, $99 a month.
  • You want the site live in 48 hours with a live walkthrough call.
  • You want new SEO blog posts every month without asking.
  • You want your website separate from your software, so switching tools later does not mean losing your site.

Can you use both?

Yes. Keep Housecall Pro for dispatching, invoicing and payments, and add your Housecall Pro online booking link to your SitesThatBook site so customers can still book straight into your schedule.

What SitesThatBook includes

$349 setup and $99 a month covers your 5-page site written for your trade, hosting, SSL, your domain connected, 2 content updates and 2 SEO blog posts every month and a monthly traffic report. No contracts, and your setup fee is refunded if you do not love the site on the launch call and we cannot fix it there.
