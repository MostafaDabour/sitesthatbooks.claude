Jobber website vs SitesThatBook

Jobber includes a website builder with its field service software. Here is an honest look at when that is enough and when a done-for-you site makes more sense.

Choose Jobber if

  • You already pay for Jobber and want a basic site without another bill.
  • You want web requests to land directly in Jobber with no extra setup.
  • You are comfortable spending time editing the site yourself.

Choose SitesThatBook if

  • You want the site written and built for you, without doing it yourself.
  • You want pages written around each service and city you cover, so you show up in more local searches.
  • You want fresh SEO content published every month without thinking about it.
  • You want your website to stay yours even if you change scheduling software later.

Can you use both?

Yes. Jobber is excellent at scheduling, quotes, invoicing and payments. Your SitesThatBook site can link to your Jobber online request form or client hub, so you keep your Jobber workflow and still get a site built to win the job.

Questions

Is the Jobber website builder free?

Jobber says its website builder is included with every Jobber plan at no additional cost. You still need a paid Jobber plan. Check Jobber's pricing page for current plan prices.

Can I use SitesThatBook if I use Jobber?

Yes. We build your website, and you keep using Jobber for scheduling, quotes and invoicing. We can link your site to your Jobber request form.

Which one is better for SEO?

Both give you a site Google can read. The difference is effort: with Jobber you write and expand the content yourself, while SitesThatBook writes service and city content for you and adds 2 SEO blog posts every month.
